## When to read what

- **Session 01 — Orientation:** Book titles and the role of each resource. Introductory chapters are optional background; no required reading, coding, or submission.
- **Session 02 — Setup and Dart exposure:** Cookbook Chapter 1 as a conceptual reference, paired with [current Flutter installation instructions](https://docs.flutter.dev/get-started/install).
- **Session 03 — Dart basics:** Dart foundations from Abu Al-Maati and Bailey/Biessek, checked against [dart.dev](https://dart.dev/language).
- **Session 04 — First Flutter run:** Flutter Cookbook Chapter 1/2 and Flutter Apprentice Chapter 2 as supplementary support, checked against [official Flutter learning](https://docs.flutter.dev/learn).
- **Later sessions:** Specific chapters will be named in each Codelab; do not assume every recipe or API in books remains unchanged.

## Source hierarchy

1. The **approved syllabus** determines what is taught and assessed.
2. These **books** provide background explanations, examples and alternative teaching approaches.
3. The **official Flutter / Dart documentation** is used to check the current API behavior, installation requirements and recent changes.

## Copyright and access

Book chapters and full PDFs are **not included** in this public repository. Links open official publishers or authorized book pages; some may require purchase or institutional access. Students should use copies made available with the appropriate permissions.
