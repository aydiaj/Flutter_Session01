Visual attribution and accuracy

All SVG diagrams, app illustrations, mascot-student artwork, the widget explorer and the color-change simulation are original to this lesson. Example screens are conceptual illustrations, not screenshots of the named commercial apps and not generated by Flutter.

Material and Cupertino note: these design systems can be used through separately published packages. Older material may use Flutter SDK bundled libraries, and formal deprecation is staged. This first orientation deliberately defers imports and migration commands.

Course delivery: no installation, coding, assignment or submission is required for Session 1.
